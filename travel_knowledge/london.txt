LONDON TRAVEL GUIDE

City: London
Country: United Kingdom
Description: London is the capital and largest city of both England and the United Kingdom, situated along the River Thames.
Popular Attractions: Tower of London, Big Ben & Houses of Parliament, British Museum, London Eye, Buckingham Palace, Hyde Park, Covent Garden
Public Transportation: Extensive transport system managed by TfL, including the London Underground (the Tube), buses, Overground trains, and river buses.
Best Time to Visit: May to September, Late November to December
Budget Traveler Daily Cost: 80 to 150 GBP
Mid-Range Traveler Daily Cost: 180 to 300 GBP
Airports: London Heathrow (LHR), London Gatwick (LGW), London Stansted (STN), London Luton (LTN)
