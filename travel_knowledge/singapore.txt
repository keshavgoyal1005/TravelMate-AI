SINGAPORE TRAVEL GUIDE

City: Singapore
Country: Singapore
Description: Singapore is a global financial center and island city-state located in Southeast Asia, off the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula.
Popular Attractions: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Jewel Changi Airport, Sentosa Island, Universal Studios Singapore, Chinatown, Singapore Botanic Gardens
Public Transportation: Efficient mass transit network dominated by MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) subway system and public buses.
Best Time to Visit: November to August (Year-round destination)
Budget Traveler Daily Cost: 100 to 150 SGD
Mid-Range Traveler Daily Cost: 180 to 280 SGD
Airports: Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)
