Tokyo Travel Guide

Tokyo is the capital of Japan and one of the world's largest
metropolitan areas.

Popular attractions include Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo Tower,
Senso-ji Temple, Meiji Shrine, Shinjuku, Akihabara, and
the Imperial Palace.

Tokyo has an extensive and efficient public transportation
system consisting mainly of trains and subway lines.

The best periods to visit Tokyo are generally March to May
and October to November.

Budget travelers may spend around 8,000 to 15,000 Japanese
yen per day excluding flights.

Mid-range travelers may spend around 15,000 to 30,000 yen
per day.

Tokyo is served primarily by Haneda Airport and Narita
International Airport.
