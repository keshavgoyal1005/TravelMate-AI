DUBAI TRAVEL GUIDE

City: Dubai
Country: United Arab Emirates
Description: Dubai is the largest and most populous city in the United Arab Emirates, located on the southeast coast of the Persian Gulf.
Popular Attractions: Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, Museum of the Future, Dubai Frame, Dubai Fountain
Public Transportation: Modern network operated by RTA, consisting of Dubai Metro, buses, trams, and water taxis (abras).
Best Time to Visit: November to March
Budget Traveler Daily Cost: 250 to 450 AED
Mid-Range Traveler Daily Cost: 600 to 1,200 AED
Airports: Dubai International Airport (DXB), Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC)
