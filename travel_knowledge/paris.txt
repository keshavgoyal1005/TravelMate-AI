Paris Travel Guide

Paris is the capital and largest city of France.

Popular attractions include the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum,
Notre-Dame Cathedral, Arc de Triomphe, Champs-Élysées,
Montmartre, and the Palace of Versailles.

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most recognizable landmarks
in the world.

The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest museums and
contains famous artworks including the Mona Lisa.

Montmartre is known for its artistic history and the
Sacré-Cœur Basilica.

The best periods to visit Paris are generally April to June
and September to October because the weather is usually
pleasant.

Paris has an extensive public transportation system including
the Metro, buses, RER trains, and trams.

Budget travelers can typically spend around 50 to 80 euros
per day excluding flights.

Mid-range travelers may spend approximately 100 to 200 euros
per day depending on accommodation and activities.

Paris is well connected internationally through Charles de
Gaulle Airport and Orly Airport.
